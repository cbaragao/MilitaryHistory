Vietnam Hamlet Assessment Survey Analysis

Chart Description (Accessibility)

Alt Text: Horizontal stacked bar chart of Vietnam hamlet assessment survey responses where Security Assessment (Night) shows 71% secure conditions, Security Assessment (Day) shows 44.5% secure with 30.8% contested areas, and quality assessments (Infrastructure, Services, Economic Development, Social Development) predominantly show good to fair conditions with Infrastructure Quality having the highest positive ratings at 62% good.

Key Features:

  • Diverging color scheme: Blue (positive) → Gray (neutral) → Red (negative)
  • Excludes unknown values (codes 0, 8, 9) for data clarity
  • Uses colorblind-safe Paul Tol color palette
  • Interactive tooltips provide detailed information

Data Source

Vietnam Special Studies Group (VSSG) hamlet evaluation reports from the National Archives. This analysis covers security assessments (day/night) and quality assessments across infrastructure, services, economic development, and social development categories.
