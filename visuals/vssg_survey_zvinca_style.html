VSSG Survey Analysis - Zvinca Style Implementation

Daniel Zvinca Approach

Three-Panel Design: Following Zvinca's methodology, this visualization separates responses into three distinct panels:

  • Left Panel (Red/Orange): Negative conditions - Enemy controlled, Poor, Contested
  • Center Panel (Gray): Neutral responses - Fair assessments
  • Right Panel (Blue): Positive conditions - Secure, Excellent, Good

Benefits:

  • Clear visual separation prevents cognitive overload
  • Easy balance estimation by comparing panel sizes
  • Questions remain aligned across all panels
  • Natural reading flow: negative → neutral → positive

Key Insights from Three-Panel View

This approach makes it immediately clear that:

  • Security assessments show minimal negative conditions but significant positive ratings
  • Quality assessments are overwhelmingly positive across all categories
  • Balance estimation is easier - positive conditions clearly dominate
  • Question comparison is straightforward with aligned y-axes

Data Source

Vietnam Special Studies Group (VSSG) hamlet evaluation reports from the National Archives. Analysis excludes unknown values (codes 0, 8, 9) for data clarity.
